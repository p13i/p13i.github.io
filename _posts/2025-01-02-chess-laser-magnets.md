My first attempt at a laser-cut chessboard was almost
[six years ago](./posts/2019/03/wooden-chess/). Oblivious to
how laser cutters optimally operate, I cut through 1/2 inch
plywood, nearly charring all the pieces. Since then, I've
made various improvements to the design of the board and to
my own craftsmanship resulting in a beautiful board with
pieces that fit perfectly and magnets to neatly secure the
board (lid) to the base (for storing the pieces).
