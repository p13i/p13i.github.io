Beams is a small 2D acoustic simulation. Three tones (C, E,
G) play from the marked points and reach the listener by the
paths drawn on the canvas, up to two wall bounces.

Click the canvas to start the audio. Drag to steer the
listener, arrow keys to move, 1 to 4 to switch scenes, R to
reset, B to switch between the microphone array and a single
omni listener.
