Three sound sources (C, E, G), a few walls, and a listener.
Each tone reaches the listener along the paths drawn on the
canvas, with up to two bounces off the walls, and the audio
is computed from those paths.

Click the canvas to start the audio. Drag to steer the
listener, arrow keys to move, 1 to 4 to switch scenes, R to
reset, B to switch between the microphone array and a single
omni listener.
